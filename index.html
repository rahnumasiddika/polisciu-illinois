Causal Inference for Observational Studies

Open for enrollment

Methods for drawing causal conclusions from observational data: matching, difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, natural experiments, synthetic controls, and related sensitivity analyses. Students work with real datasets in R. Key texts include Rosenbaum's Design of Observational Studies and An Introduction to the Theory of Observational Studies, and Cunningham's Causal Inference: The Mixtape.

How to Enroll

Pick the case that fits you.

Illinois student, course taught at Illinois

  1. Find the course number and CRN in the course listing above.
  2. Register through Illinois's usual graduate registration system.
  3. If registration is restricted, contact [Graduate Program Coordinator, email] for permission.

Illinois student, course taught at another university

  1. Talk with your advisor about whether the course fits your program.
  2. Email the instructor to say you plan to join from Illinois.
  3. Email the Illinois PoliSci U coordinator. They will give you the Illinois course number to register under, so the credit appears on your Illinois record. [Confirm: local course number, e.g. PS 590 section]
  4. Get the video or Zoom link from the instructor before the first class.

Student at Ohio State, Minnesota or Wisconsin, course taught at Illinois

  1. Ask your department's PoliSci U contact or graduate office which local course number to register under. At Wisconsin, for example, PoliSci U courses have been listed as POLI SCI 919.
  2. Register at your home university.
  3. Email the instructor so you get the video link and course materials.

About PoliSci U

PoliSci U in Advanced Political Methodology is a cooperative graduate training program run by the political science departments at Illinois, Minnesota, Ohio State, and Wisconsin–Madison. Departments share advanced methods courses over interactive video, so students can take specialized courses their own department may not offer that year. The program began in 1995–96 as an experiment in multi-site video teaching among Big Ten (then CIC) political science departments, led by W. Phillips Shively of Minnesota.

Past course schedules and papers on the program are on the Ohio State PoliSci U page.
